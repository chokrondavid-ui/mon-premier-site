    Hole 04 — The walk

    Eighteen holes. No strain.

    Three wheels and a metal frame carry the whole bag. You walk the course with one hand on the bar; the weight stays on the ground.

    A support at the base and fastening straps at the top hold your bag firmly, so it doesn't twist on a slope or rattle on the path.

    Press the release and fold the handle: the cart closes in seconds, down to 92.5 × 30.5 × 31.5 cm. It goes in a boot, a garage or a locker.

    Set the handle to your height with the lever, roll on large smooth wheels, and lock it with the one-step foot brake when the tee box slopes.
